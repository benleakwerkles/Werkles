# Skybro — Formation Operating Brief value rewrite

## Strongest free success moment

The member completes the browser-local Formation practice session and copies a
clean, plain-text Werkle Operating Brief. It captures only explicit, mutually
selected wording from the session and gives prospective collaborators a useful
foundation for their first real operational conversation without claiming
formal enforcement, holding data hostage, or requiring account creation.

## Five-section structure

1. **Mutual operational purpose:** the immediate shared objective, assembled
   only from exact mutual wording.
2. **Working cadence and availability:** aligned expectations about weekly time,
   working windows, and response expectations.
3. **Operational authority and discretion:** the decisions each person may make
   independently and which require consultation.
4. **Communication and review protocols:** check-in rhythm and a process for
   raising friction early.
5. **Orderly pause and exit steps:** non-punitive expectations for pausing or
   stepping away, expressed only when members authored and accepted them.

## Non-silent revisiting triggers

- Scope expansion, such as adding a service line or materially changing time.
- A shared-resource milestone, such as acquiring meaningful tools/equipment.
- An availability shift in employment, schedule, or working area.

These triggers invite review; they do not alter the brief automatically.
