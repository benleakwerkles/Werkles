## Product problem

The `company-starter-floor` lesson currently tells a member to sort tasks into decided, professional review, and unknown, but supplies no board, no plain distinction between state structure and federal tax treatment, and no direct path to authoritative starting sources. That is generic advice, not a sellable work product.

## Candidate

Add a six-part launch board with explicit statuses, notes/source/deadline prompts, unresolved counts, device save, copy, and clear. Explain the LLC/S-corporation distinction narrowly from current SBA and IRS primary sources, link directly to those sources, and keep all member-specific choices with independent state/legal/tax review.

## Hard edges

- Educational planning aid only; no entity, tax, legal, filing, deadline, or eligibility conclusion.
- No state-specific claim without the member's state and authoritative state source.
- No form submission, provider recommendation, account save, automatic sharing, or professional impersonation.
- Exact-shape local storage validation; no network write.
- No production, schema, provider, secret, payment, push, or deploy action.
