# V — Distinct Ghost conversations

Date: 2026-08-20  
Seat: Heimerdinker / Codex Foreman  
Lane: local Ghost matching walkthrough

## Vision

Three differently ranked practice profiles should not produce the same four
generic conversation buttons. The interaction should make each profile's
specific contribution, need, uncertainty, and likely first working test
visible without inventing dialogue or exposing private profile fields.

## Pulled judgment

- Operator: the prior matches felt like the same lane having the same
  conversation; last-spoken phrasing must not be canonized merely to please the
  Operator.
- Ender: members should not need insider vocabulary before receiving value.
- Bean: interaction DTOs must stay narrow; raw profile material and exact
  location must not leak beyond the reviewed projection.

## G ideas

1. Derive four member-specific, plain-language question labels from the already
   sanitized interaction DTO: contribution, reciprocal need, first small test,
   and unresolved check.
2. Give the first-test answer a lane-specific working shape while keeping it a
   practice conversation, not an introduction or promise.
3. Prove different profiles receive different visible question sets and voices,
   while the DTO remains free of raw synthetic story, files, seats, or private
   fields.

## Hard edges

- No random output, LLM, external send, real-member contact, provider call,
  persistence, schema, secret, push, deploy, or spend.
- Do not reveal new profile fields; use only the existing sanitized DTO.
- Do not imply availability, verification, identity, or real introduction.

## Stop condition

Stop after focused interaction/diversity/location proofs, TypeScript, and a
local browser walk are green, or at a true human gate.
