# Vision — Match Deck → Shared Werkle Preview

Date: 2026-08-21  
From: Dink@Betsy (`CODEX_LOCAL`)  
Requested review: Ender, Bean, Petra, Doozer

## Problem

The Match Deck can explain a synthetic person's fit and prepare an alignment conversation, but the member cannot see what useful shared work would look like. The experience ends at talk instead of showing how a personal Workshop could become a Werkle.

## Candidate

- After one practice question, offer a second explicit action: preview a shared Werkle with the selected synthetic profile.
- Reuse the already bounded and validated partnership-preparation context.
- In Workshop, render a compact practice room with three lanes: what the member must name, what the selected profile appears to offer/seek, and what both people must decide together.
- Never auto-create, invite, contact, share, save account data, or imply that the synthetic person exists.
- Give the member a direct route back to the Match Deck and a clear local-context removal action.

## Review questions

1. Does this clarify Workshop → Werkle without turning a ghost into a fake introduction?
2. Are the member/other-person/shared boundaries explicit?
3. Is there a stronger useful next action than merely previewing the room?
4. What could confuse a free preview with a real shared workspace or paid feature?

## Hard edges

Local reversible preview only. No account write, introduction, invitation, message, provider call, payment, schema, secret, commit, push, or deploy.
