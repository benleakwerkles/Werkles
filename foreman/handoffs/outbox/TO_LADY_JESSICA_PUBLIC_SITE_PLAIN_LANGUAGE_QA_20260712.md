# TO LADY JESSICA - Public Site Plain-Language QA

## Mission

Review the customer journey as a first-time visitor. Flag copy that sounds like an engineering console, exposes internal names, or asks customers to understand implementation details.

## Acceptance test

A visitor should understand:

1. what Werkles does;
2. what information they are being asked for;
3. what happens next;
4. what is unavailable without being shown internal deployment mechanics;
5. who controls consequential actions, described by role rather than a founder's personal name.

Return findings into the public-content cleanup receipt. No deploy or push.
