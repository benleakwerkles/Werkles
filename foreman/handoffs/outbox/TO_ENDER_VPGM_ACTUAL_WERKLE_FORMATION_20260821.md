Review the complete vision below as an actual member experience. Do not code. Return `PASS`, `PATCH`, or `BLOCK` and provide exact replacement copy/interaction changes.

## Product truth

Two people have matched. Each owns a private Workshop. They are considering a shared Werkle. Werkles must help them decide what information and commitments enter the shared room without overwriting either person's source work or pretending a legal company exists.

## Proposed flow

1. Consent to compare; a match does not create a Werkle.
2. Side-by-side source statements.
3. Per-subject choices: Carry forward, Combine into an editable joint statement, Keep private, Park the disagreement.
4. Shared draft: purpose, customer/use case, 30-day test, responsibilities, contributions, decision rights, money questions, proof needs, unknowns.
5. Counts for agreement, rewrite, privacy, and disagreement—no compatibility score.
6. Either person can revise or stop; originals remain.
7. Bellows help appears next to the friction it addresses.

Attack:

- Where does this make either person feel tricked, steamrolled, exposed, over-lawyered, or falsely safe?
- What should the four decision labels actually say in natural English?
- What one disagreement should be deliberately seeded so the walkthrough proves Werkles can handle tension?
- Write the human copy for the transition from two Workshops to one proposed Werkle.
- What would make this worth paying $9.99/month for instead of using a shared document?

Hard edges: synthetic profile, local walkthrough, no real invite/message/entity/agreement. End with an explicit terminal verdict.
