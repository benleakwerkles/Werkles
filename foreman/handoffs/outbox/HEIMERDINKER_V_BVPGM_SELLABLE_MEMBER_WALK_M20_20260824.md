# Heimerdinker V — BVPGM Sellable Member Walk M20

## Larger checkpoint

Make the current local Werkles member journey feel coherent, remembered,
substantial, human, and sellable from the homepage through the first proposed
Werkle—while Ben walks it as `gimprobotester`.

## Workstreams

1. Journey continuity: Home → Login → Intake → Recommendations → Workshop →
   Match Deck → Formation → Personal Bellows / Crucible / Membership.
2. Account custody: the signed-in member and their latest Intake, working
   artifacts, selected match, Formation draft, and return path must not silently
   become an unrelated bakery demo or disappear.
3. Recommendation value: specific reasoning, useful work products, and next
   doors—not mirrored Intake text or scraps of generic advice.
4. Matching value: varied ranked candidates, human conversation, proximity,
   visible reasoning, and revisitable comparison without wealth ranking.
5. Formation value: two-sided synthetic personalities and a shared company
   negotiation that helps expose expectations instead of pretending agreement.
6. Bellows depth: public discovery plus personal, revisitable lessons that move
   live Workshop / Match / Werkle work forward.
7. Tech-stack readiness: honest local scaffolds and stopping boundaries for
   Stripe, Plaid, Twilio, identity, email, storage, and later policy-gated
   providers. No provider activation in this cycle.
8. Presentation: consistent public/member headers, plain human copy, readable
   contrast, and visual pauses so pages do not feel like homework.

## Acceptance floor

- The homepage clearly opens the member journey.
- Login returns to the intended destination and does not fake a message inbox.
- The current member's Intake is the source of recommendations and matches.
- A user can reach multiple varied matches and form a revisitable practice
  Werkle with one of them.
- The Formation partner has coherent synthetic opinions and cannot be
  impersonated by the user.
- Personal Bellows and Crucible have useful next work, not dead ends.
- Every route loads, the member navigation stays consistent, mobile text is
  readable, and no console-breaking error appears.
