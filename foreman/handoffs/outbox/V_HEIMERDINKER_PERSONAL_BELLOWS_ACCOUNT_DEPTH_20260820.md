# V — Personal Bellows account depth

**Outcome:** My Bellows follows the authenticated member's latest Intake and turns each selected lesson into a short, complete three-step working sequence—not a single generic tip.

## Strongest ideas

1. Use the authenticated Recommendations endpoint as the Personal Bellows account source; browser/demo failure must be visible, never substituted.
2. Preserve up to three distinct lessons, but include all three bounded exercises and their outputs so the member can actually do the work.

## Hard edges

- Public lessons receive no member answers.
- No LLM/provider call, invented fact, permanent curriculum claim, account monitoring claim, or raw Intake wall.
- No SQL apply, env/secret action, deploy, push, or spend.
