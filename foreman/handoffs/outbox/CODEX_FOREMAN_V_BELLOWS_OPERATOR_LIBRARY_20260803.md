## Vision

Bellows stops being a hallway that describes education and becomes a useful
operator library. A visitor should be able to open one door, learn where a
popular business idea breaks, and leave with better questions for the people
whose professional judgment they may hire.

The first floor has four doors:

1. Guru Busting — critique repeatable patterns, never personalities.
2. Company Starter Kits — staged checklists that organize a launch without
   pretending to replace attorneys, accountants, lenders, insurers, or local
   authorities.
3. Proof Before Reliance — distinguish claims, documents, provider checks,
   currency, scope, and unresolved gaps.
4. Partnership Alignment — surface expectations about roles, money, authority,
   time, exits, deadlock, and change before anyone drafts final agreements.

## Limits

- framework-level educational content only
- no named-person attacks, legal conclusions, personalized recommendations,
  guaranteed outcomes, unverified market statistics, or invented evidence
- no prices or paid SKU promises
- legal-adjacent starter materials remain questions/checklists, not contracts
- no Crypto / Alt-Asset pillar in this slice
- no provider calls, intake opening, schema, SQL, RLS, or production writes

## Stop condition

Stop after a local reviewable curriculum floor, focused truth/accessibility
proof, typecheck/build, CBCC packets, and receipt. Push, deploy, publication,
legal approval, and promotion remain separate gates.
