# Werkles BVPGM M12 — Unresolved Decision → Real Test → Useful Return

Date: 2026-08-23  
Foreman: Heimerdinker@Betsy  
Status: VISION AUTHORED — local candidate only

## Larger checkpoint

Make a Werkle continue helping after two people agree on wording. The next
unresolved decision should lead to one concrete, same-week test; the result
should return to Personal Bellows; and Crucible should remain optional unless a
narrow external fact could actually change that test.

## Distinct workstreams

1. **Formation:** translate the next unresolved joint decision into a small
   observable experiment without inventing an owner, deadline, promise, legal
   term, financing choice, or partner consent.
2. **Personal Bellows:** let members return to the accepted decision, the test
   they proposed, and what happened—without importing private predictions or
   silently treating a proposal as mutual agreement.
3. **Crucible / provider boundary:** name when outside evidence is unnecessary,
   and when one dated provider check could change the decision. Never turn a
   check into match quality, character, wealth rank, or broad trust.
4. **Copy and human rhythm:** keep the experience conversational, legible, and
   specific; remove homework/internal-control language and avoid another long
   wall of bubbles.

## V notification obligation

Packet creation is not notification. The Foreman must attempt the established
background route for every addressed seat, record delivery and route failures
separately, and count no review until a terminal receipt is harvested and read.

## Hard edges

- No Codex subagents or new environments.
- No foreground mouse, keyboard, cursor, or clipboard use.
- No provider call, activation, credentials, account settings, secrets, spend,
  production data, schema/RLS/SQL, push, deploy, or release approval.
- No generated partner answer may become their real answer.
- No proposed test becomes an assignment or agreement without explicit mutual
  acceptance.
- No external source becomes an endorsement or personalized professional advice.

## Stop condition

Stop after a bounded local implementation, focused member walk, exact receipt,
post-build CBCC rotation, and source-bound candidate reseal—or earlier at a
true Human Gate.
