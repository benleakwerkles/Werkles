## Vision

Werkles must visibly do three different jobs:

1. **Hear:** retain the member's exact words in a collapsed, editable audit
   trail.
2. **Interpret:** name the tension, asymmetry, or hidden dependency that the
   member did not already state.
3. **Advance:** produce a bounded experiment, artifact, and decision rule that
   changes what the member can do next.

Member prose is evidence. It is never the finished insight, headline, or action.

## Current worked case

The member is building Werkles and PookaKind simultaneously, is a first-time
software builder, is testing both, wants customers and funding, and is deciding
whether the products are ready for mentors/investors or should first become
customer-ready.

The non-obvious synthesis is not “name the decision.” It is:

- there are two products and at least three different audiences (customer,
  mentor, investor);
- “ready” means different evidence to each audience;
- polishing both products at once can consume time without resolving which one
  has the stronger commercial signal;
- therefore the first useful move is an asymmetric proof sprint: choose one
  product and one audience, expose it to a fixed number of outsiders, record
  observable behavior, and use a written go/rework/park rule.

## G ideas

1. Add a deterministic digital-product readiness interpretation that turns the
   saved signals into a concise diagnosis, three falsifiable insights, and a
   seven-day proof sprint. Give each of the three ranked options a genuinely
   different job.
2. Remove verbatim member paragraphs from prominent plan headings and action
   steps. Keep exact words only in the existing collapsed trace.
3. Add hostile fixtures proving that the result contains synthesis and distinct
   option actions while never copying the long decision as a title or task.
