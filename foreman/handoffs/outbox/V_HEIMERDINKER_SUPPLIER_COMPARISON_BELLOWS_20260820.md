# V — Supplier Comparison Bellows

**Outcome:** Equipment and supplier recommendations open a practical lesson with a same-requirement comparison tool, not “get three quotes” advice.

## Strongest ideas

1. Teach requirement-first comparison: exact job/spec/date, comparable first-year cost, seller evidence, service/downtime, and contradiction checks.
2. Add an in-browser three-option comparison card that calculates only user-entered costs and copies a working brief; it ranks no supplier and stores/sends nothing.

## Sources

- Current SBA planning guidance on one-time/monthly costs and checking comparable costs with vendors/service providers.
- FTC small-business scam guidance on equipment leasing, fine print, rushed signatures, and unsupported claims.

## Hard edges

- No provider ranking, endorsement, legal/financial advice, paid-placement implication, network submission, or persistent save.
