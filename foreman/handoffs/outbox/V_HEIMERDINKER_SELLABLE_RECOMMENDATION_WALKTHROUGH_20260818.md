## Vision

One saved Intake must produce something a member could reasonably pay to use:

1. a plain recommendation stated as a decision, not a category;
2. the exact Intake facts that caused it;
3. a tailored short experiment or work plan;
4. a reusable artifact with a measurable decision rule;
5. the next Werkles surface, person, or comparison lane that becomes useful
   after the artifact exists.

The walkthrough fails if it merely repeats the Intake, says to clarify or
research something, or decorates generic advice with a score.

## Pulled actual-CBCC judgment

- Bean: preserve explicit goal, blocker, assets, constraints, path status, and
  unknowns; cite the words that raised or excluded a path; do not treat scores
  as probability or success.
- Ender: first value must arrive before profile extraction; one sectioned page
  is safer than routed steps while custody is local; stop collecting only
  deficits and make the output visibly causal.
- Petra: a sellable Bellows result is a reusable artifact separating the claim,
  evidence, inference, gap, and next bounded check. Generic skepticism is not a
  product.
- Computer/Thufir + Bean recommendation-path review: the path is recommendation
  → playbook → artifact → Bellows lesson → evidence-labeled comparison; no
  supplier may be called best without locality, eligibility, source, freshness,
  price, availability, conflict, and commercial-disclosure evidence.
- Swanson + Orson/Doozer: keep the pithy acknowledgment and option-specific
  language; remove full Intake echo before value; exact-source candidate passed
  the narrow member-facing language review.

## Triple G

1. Build a deterministic, Intake-grounded decision plan that can combine
   signals across fields (for example testing-stage + customer demand +
   equipment pressure) instead of treating every recommendation kind in
   isolation.
2. Prefill a useful action board and decision rule from the member's own facts,
   with editable unknowns rather than blank generic homework.
3. Recompose the first selected readout so the recommendation, reason, sprint,
   and finish line appear before governance/explanation detail.

## Hard edges

- Local only; no production mutation.
- No provider calls, named supplier rankings, scraped directory, or invented
  market facts.
- No secrets, SQL/schema/RLS apply, spend, push, deploy, or publish.
- No Codex subagents or new environments.
- Actual CBCC packets above are inputs; outgoing requests alone are never
  counted as reviews.

## Stop condition

Stop after the saved local Intake produces and browser-proves a materially
specific decision plan and reusable artifact, or when the next step requires
current provider research, account custody, or another true human gate.
