# V — Personal Bellows and one Pooka per Werkle

Date: 2026-08-20
Foreman: Heimerdinker / Dink
Execution context: CODEX_LOCAL on Betsy/Windows

## Vision

Split Bellows into two legible products:

1. **Public Bellows** — the full lesson, toolkit, template, and starter-kit catalog anyone can browse or buy.
2. **Personal Bellows** — an owner-bound learning floor generated from a member's Intake, Recommendations, saved work artifacts, Workshop state, and explicit Werkle activity.

Each new Werkle may receive one persistent Pooka guide. The Pooka is randomly selected from an approved animal/body archetype, then dressed and equipped from a controlled vocation/style system appropriate to the Werkle: lab coat for science, scrubs for medical, suit for corporate/commercial, shop gear for trades, and so on. The character must persist for the life of the Werkle instead of rerandomizing on refresh.

## Product outcome

A member following “Learn how” from a tailored recommendation should land on a lesson that:

- names the recommendation and work artifact that caused it to appear;
- summarizes the member's situation without parroting raw answers;
- teaches the general method with examples selected for this Werkle;
- produces an editable artifact or checklist;
- offers relevant public Bellows modules, suppliers, professionals, comparisons, or paid Werkles services;
- records progress only after explicit member action; and
- explains what is personal, shared with Werkle participants, or public.

## Hard edges

- No silent monitoring of private member conversations.
- No inference from a message, call, or session unless the participants were told what is analyzed and consented to that exact use.
- A shared Werkle lesson must distinguish private-member material from participant-shared material.
- No financial, legal, tax, medical, or credential claim may be presented as personalized professional advice without the correct qualified source and disclosure.
- Generated Pookas may guide, explain, and keep continuity. They do not impersonate a human professional or provider.
- A generated image is a draft asset until approved; no Ghost Forge spend or batch is authorized by this packet.
- No push, deploy, provider call, secret access, schema/RLS change, or production-data mutation.
