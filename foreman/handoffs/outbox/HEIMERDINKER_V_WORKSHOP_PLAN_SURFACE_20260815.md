## Product decision

The Workshop is the editable plan between discovery and introductions. The
member flow is:

1. Intake names the goal and bottleneck.
2. Workshop reflects the answers back, exposes gaps, and shows the workbench.
3. Recommendations compare plausible next moves.
4. Intros become relevant only when a person is actually the next move.

The current route is too thin and presents Intake, Intros, and Proof as equal
doors. This round will replace that fork with a clear sequence and a useful
empty/populated Workshop preview.
