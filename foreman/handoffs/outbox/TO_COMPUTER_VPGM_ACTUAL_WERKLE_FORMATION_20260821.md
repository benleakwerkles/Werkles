## Product truth

Werkles matches people first. Each person has a private Workshop. A Werkle begins only when two or more people deliberately choose to share work. Werkles does not form the legal entity, broker a transaction, negotiate for them, or replace independent legal/tax advice.

## Proposed state model

For each topic, retain both source statements and record one formation state:

- carry forward unchanged;
- combine into a jointly edited statement;
- keep private in the source Workshop;
- park as an unresolved disagreement.

The shared draft covers purpose, first customer/use case, 30-day test, roles, contributions, decision rights, money questions, proof needs, and unknowns. Originals are immutable. No compatibility score. Contextual Bellows tools appear beside relevant friction.
