## Larger checkpoint

A promising match must become a useful, revisitable working relationship—not a
one-time quiz followed by a twelve-question worksheet. M11 joins four distinct
workstreams:

1. **Formation:** turn the next unresolved topic into a small joint decision
   with an understandable consequence.
2. **Match Deck:** make conversation practice produce information that can
   improve the later Werkle without silently changing either profile.
3. **Personal Bellows:** reopen useful lessons and saved work from unresolved
   formation questions, not only from Intake.
4. **Crucible/provider boundary:** identify the narrow check that could unblock
   one mutual decision without ranking either person.
