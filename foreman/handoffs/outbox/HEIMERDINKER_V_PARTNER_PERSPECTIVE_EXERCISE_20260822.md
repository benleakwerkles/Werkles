# HEIMERDINKER V — Partner Perspective Exercise

Date: 2026-08-22  
Issuer: Heimerdinker@Betsy, Werkles Foreman  
Surface: `/dashboard/werkles/formation?candidate=ghost_095`

## Vision

Turn the practice-only `Review as you / Review as the other member` switch into a valuable partner-alignment exercise without ever allowing one real member to author, alter, or impersonate the other member's actual position.

Each participant privately predicts the other person's expectations, then answers for themselves. Werkles compares prediction with self-report and turns the gaps into an explicit conversation before shared company language is accepted.

## Why

Early partner disputes often grow from unspoken differences about time, money, authority, compensation, reinvestment, distributions, risk, profitability timelines, and exit. Werkles should help expose those differences while they are still discussable.

## Proposed local practice slice

- Preserve the tester-only actor switch for the synthetic member walkthrough.
- Clearly separate `Their actual answer` from `Your private prediction of their answer`.
- Add a focused expectation exercise covering weekly time, personal cash, first-pay expectations, decision authority, reinvestment/distributions, and a fair exit.
- Show a plain-language gap map: aligned, close enough to discuss, or different enough to stop and clarify.
- Provide one useful conversation prompt per gap.
- Keep predictions private and non-authoritative. They never enter the Werkle or become attributed to the other person.
- Only separately entered, exact mutual decisions may enter shared company language.

## Hard edges

No impersonation, mind-reading claim, relationship score, psychological diagnosis, legal/tax/financial advice, real-member contact, account persistence claim, schema, provider, secrets, push, deploy, or spend.
