# V — BVPGM Multi-Location Opportunity Network

Date: 2026-09-01  
From: Heimerdinker / Codex Foreman  
Execution context: CODEX_LOCAL on BETSY  
Larger checkpoint: make real-resource discovery useful to a Werkle whose members, work, customers, and possible meeting points may be in different places.

## Operator correction

Decatur was an example, not a durable user location. The product must not freeze a city from conversation into canon. A member in Atlanta and a collaborator in Columbus, Georgia need separate local results, fair meeting options, remote-capable resources, and explicit travel constraints.

## Workstreams

1. **Location model** — member locations, project/service area, travel radius, remote/local preference, and optional shared meeting region remain distinct.
2. **Query planning** — generate participant-local, project-local, midpoint/meeting, statewide/remote, and official-jurisdiction searches without silently averaging people into one place.
3. **Opportunity comparison** — label whom/what each result serves; never imply that geographic closeness means quality or fit.
4. **Three-room use** — Recommendations previews relevant categories, Workshop compares/saves/rejects options, shared Werkle lets participants discuss and assign investigation.
5. **Trust and experience review** — Bean attacks privacy/inference/financial-ranking risk; Lady Jessica attacks visual density, geographic comprehension, and cross-room flow.

## Hard edges

- Do not infer home address, precise location, protected traits, travel ability, finances, or willingness to relocate.
- Do not reveal one member's private location to another without deliberate sharing.
- Do not call a place central, convenient, nearby, serviceable, available, zoned, affordable, or suitable without source and calculation evidence.
- No provider activation, keys, billing, persistence, schema/RLS, push, or deploy.
- Decatur fixture remains a dated test case, never a default member identity.

## Completion proof

- Multi-location contract and planner tests for Atlanta + Columbus.
- A review surface demonstrates participant-local, shared-meeting, and statewide/remote lanes.
- Exact CBCC dispatch/response/validation status recorded separately.
