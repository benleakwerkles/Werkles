## Larger checkpoint

Make the new Formation test loop useful after the work happens. A member should
be able to return with a result, separate observation from interpretation, name
what changed, and carry one proposed next decision into Personal Bellows—without
turning one person's notes into the partner's answer or a company agreement.

## Distinct workstreams

1. **Formation:** record what happened, what the result appears to mean, and the
   next proposed decision against the exact current accepted source and proposed
   action.
2. **Personal Bellows:** restore the valid result beside the accepted wording
   and test; make the next conversation obvious and revisitable.
3. **Provider boundary:** an outside check remains optional and claim-specific;
   a completed test must not automatically create Plaid, identity, payment,
   phone, or background work.
4. **Copy and visual rhythm:** distinguish observation, interpretation, and
   proposal with plain human language and a compact visual hierarchy.
