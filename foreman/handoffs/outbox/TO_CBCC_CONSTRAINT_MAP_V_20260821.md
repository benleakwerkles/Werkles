## Product problem

`pitch-is-not-the-plan` is the only Public Bellows lesson with no reusable work product. Its current fifteen-minute move asks for one observed stop and three possible causes, but members must create the structure themselves and can easily turn causes into favorite solutions.

## Candidate

Add a Constraint Map with one observed stop, three competing cause rows, evidence for, evidence against, one cheapest check, and a check-by date. Include explicit device save, copy, clear, and an unresolved counter. Preserve unknowns and reject solution-shaped cause wording in the instructional copy rather than pretending to validate business truth.

## Hard edges

- This is diagnosis preparation, not a diagnosis, recommendation, proof, or professional conclusion.
- No scoring, probability, automatic cause selection, provider ranking, network write, account save, or sharing.
- Device storage must validate exact shape and length before restore.
- No production, schema, provider, secret, payment, push, or deploy action.
