# Heimerdinker V — BVPGM Ghosts With Opinions M19

## Larger checkpoint

Turn the Formation studio and adjacent Ghost walk into a useful two-sided
simulation: the member answers as themself; the selected synthetic member has a
consistent fictional personality, working posture, financial scenario, and
topic-by-topic opinions the member can inspect and react to.

## Workstreams

1. Formation language: retire “Decide what crosses the line” and explain what
   the Shared Werkle actually does.
2. Ghost agency: replace member impersonation of the Ghost with explicit,
   deterministic Ghost answers and reactions.
3. Ghost depth: expose work ethic, pace, risk posture, disagreement style,
   availability, contribution expectations, and a fictional financial scenario.
4. Privacy/trust: never imply a Ghost answer is real, current, provider-verified,
   or attached to an actual bank account. Do not expose account numbers.
5. Walkability: make differences, agreement, counterproposals, and unresolved
   questions observable on desktop and 390px.

## Hard edges

- Local preview only; synthetic Ghosts only.
- No provider calls, secrets, schema/RLS, production data, spend, push, deploy,
  external release, or foreground input.
- No compatibility score or wealth ranking.
- Gate 05 remains HOLD.
