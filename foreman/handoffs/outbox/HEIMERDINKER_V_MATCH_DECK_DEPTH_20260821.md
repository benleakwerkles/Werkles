## Bounded question

How should Werkles show more than three honest ghost matches without turning the
Match Deck into a repetitive directory or weakening the evidence floor?

## Fixed baseline

- 150 synthetic practice profiles exist across six lanes.
- Matching already drops profiles with no positive reason and avoids repeated
  contribution signatures where possible.
- Three profiles are currently transported to the member surface.
- Ghost dialogue was just rewritten to sound conversational.

## Candidate shape for attack

- Transport up to nine honestly supported profiles.
- Show six initially, then reveal three more on request.
- Keep one selected conversation at a time.
- State the visible/available count.
- Never fill an empty slot with a weak profile.

## Allowed

Read-only CBCC review; bounded local TypeScript/React/CSS/test changes; localhost
walkthrough.

## Forbidden

No subagents, new tasks/environments, schema, provider changes, production data,
wealth ranking, paid calls, push, deploy, or invented review receipts.

## Acceptance

- More than three profiles are reachable when honest matches exist.
- Initial view remains scannable.
- No match is added merely to hit a count.
- Profile variety and conversational interaction remain intact.
- Empty/fewer-result truth remains explicit.
