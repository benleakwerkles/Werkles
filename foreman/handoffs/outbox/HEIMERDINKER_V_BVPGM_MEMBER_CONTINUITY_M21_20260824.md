## Larger checkpoint

Make the local member experience explain what Werkles remembers, what a future
match could see, how a possible connection becomes shared work, and where the
technology belongs—without implying that browser-local work or synthetic
profiles are live account records.

## Distinct workstreams

1. Member custody and recovery: separate account-bound Intake data from
   browser-local drafts, then give the member a clear return route.
2. Match Deck transparency: show what deliberately supplied information could
   become visible to another member before real introductions exist.
3. Match-to-Werkle continuity: keep candidate, questions, expectations, and
   next action coherent from the deck into Formation and back.
4. Bellows continuity: keep tailored lesson titles, drafts, and next doors tied
   to the current business rather than demo residue.
5. Provider readiness: keep Stripe, Plaid, Twilio, identity, storage, and later
   screening states narrow, dated, and separate from trust.
6. Presentation and copy: remove internal labels, robotic sentences, dead ends,
   and controls whose consequence is unclear.

## Acceptance floor

- The member can preview the limited project facts a future match could see;
  nothing is shared merely by opening the preview.
- Practice conversations do not claim to contact a real person.
- Saved local work names its actual storage boundary and return path.
- Match Deck, Formation, Personal Bellows, Crucible, and Membership retain the
  shared member header and produce no framework error overlay.
- Digital-product Intake cannot inherit bakery, oven, or shared-kitchen advice.
- Provider scaffolds remain test/local and do not imply production activation.
