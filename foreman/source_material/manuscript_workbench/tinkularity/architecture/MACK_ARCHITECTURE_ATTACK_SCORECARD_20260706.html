Fatal Flags

  • Claims real-time shared Aeye consciousness without a falsifiable mechanism.
  • Treats a prompt, watcher, or classifier as containment.
  • Treats sender-side packet write as delivery proof.
  • Treats transport ACK as receiver work completion.
  • Routes secrets through chat, logs, or source files.
  • Lets stale world-state become current truth.
  • Requires Ben to keep being the clipboard/message bus after claiming otherwise.
  • Cannot produce a next legal command from the cockpit.
