## Cross-room invariants

1. The global shell, typography, button grammar, and base paper/ink palette do not change by room.
2. One room accent appears at the header edge, hero edge, active navigation state, and at most one progress/transition cue.
3. Cards do not inherit multiple room colors. Content hierarchy, not rainbow coding, separates sections.
4. Every first viewport contains the room name, its one job, and the main working surface or a direct path to it.
5. Every long route has one dominant action. Secondary destinations are grouped under a quiet continuation region.
6. “Next” means the most likely continuation from the member’s current state—not a hard-coded sales funnel step.
7. Public Bellows and Personal Bellows are the same room at different depths; they must not borrow the People color identity.
8. About Me is not Workshop. Profile identity and proof controls may be adjacent, but their jobs remain distinguishable.

## Questions for review

- Should Recommendations lead first to Workshop or dynamically to the recommended destination type?
- Can About Me and Proof share one route while maintaining two clear sections, or does that repeat the current “too few pages doing too much” problem?
- Which Workshop sections are main work versus reference/continuation?
- What is the smallest consistent room cue that survives mobile without adding header clutter?
