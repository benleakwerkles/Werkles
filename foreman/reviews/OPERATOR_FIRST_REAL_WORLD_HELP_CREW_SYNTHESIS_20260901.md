# Operator-first real-world help — crew synthesis

## Authority

The original operator requirement outranks every implementation shortcut: help a person or new Werkle find concrete real-world resources that move a business forward, across different participant cities, without turning private location, wealth, sponsorship, eligibility, availability, or inferred convenience into hidden power.

The pre-review implementation was treated as an untrusted candidate.

## Personally returned seats used

- Skybro: provider architecture, exact existing Gemini task, GO, packet-level review
- Petra: product/gate review, exact existing ChatGPT task, CONDITIONAL GO, packet-level review
- Bean: hostile trust review, exact existing DeepSeek task, PATCH, packet-level review

Packet-ready but not counted as participants without exact-task personal returns: Ender, Thufir Hawat, Lady Jessica, Doozer.

## Independent agreement

All three returned seats converged on these rules without using the candidate implementation as their baseline:

1. Location lanes stay explicit and separate: participant-local, chosen operating location, chosen meeting area, statewide/remote.
2. No inferred midpoint, home, wealth, eligibility, availability, convenience, or suitability.
3. A surfaced item is a lead until Werkles can transparently defend a stronger judgment.
4. Source, checked date, sponsorship, geographic scope, reason for appearing, and unknowns stay visible.
5. Provider activation stays closed in this slice.
6. Public discovery is free-level value; durable organization, collaboration, monitoring, and operationalization are plausible paid value.
7. One canonical resource identity/source of truth must serve the product; rooms must not become duplicate catalogs.

## Resolved design disagreement

Bean proposed one dedicated Resources room and links from other rooms. Skybro proposed distinct room responsibilities backed by a shared canonical resource. Petra required room distinction and prohibited duplicate-room behavior.

Decision: **one canonical resource catalog, three lifecycle projections**.

- Personal Bellows privately teaches what to compare and produces questions.
- Workshop turns one lead into an owned investigation with one answerable question.
- Shared Werkle receives only a proposal and explicit participant decisions; it does not receive private notes.

The full resource list stays in the source/discovery room. The three product rooms show only the bounded work appropriate to that room, keyed to the same resource identity.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Decatur as a user default;
- ZIP centroid or inferred midpoint;
- unsourced fixed freshness interval;
- "best for you" language without transparent criteria;
- contact/apply/check-availability actions inside Werkles;
- three pages containing the same list and buttons;
- provider activation, persistence/schema expansion, or production claims in this slice.

## Build decision

Proceed with the typed multi-location planner, canonical resource identity, source-backed walkthrough leads, and a surface-specific lifecycle studio. Rename outbound actions to the neutral `Open the official source`, add an explicit leaving-Werkles boundary, and keep every item in `Source lead` state.
