## Agreements

1. The present instrument collects too much problem prose and too little explicit
   structure. It cannot safely distinguish a current need from a past attempt,
   a rejection, a constraint, or a vague mention.
2. The starter profile must contain only member-authored facts. Missing assets,
   skills, offers, and constraints remain `UNKNOWN`; Werkles does not guess them.
3. Structured choices must carry present/history/ruled-out semantics so negation
   is data rather than a keyword-parser gamble.
4. Recommendations must show visible causality: the member's words, the rule path,
   and what raised, lowered, or excluded each option.
5. The flow stays one sectioned page while custody is browser/session-bound. No
   routed multi-screen form until each step is durably saved.
6. Member-facing first contact uses ordinary language. `Intake`, `Werkler`, and
   workshop-metaphor vocabulary do not have to be learned before value appears.

## Tension and ruling

Ender would earn matching-specific questions after the first useful readout;
Bean requires assets and offers before matching can be honest. Ruling: collect
assets as an optional third section before the readout, and invite the member's
offer there without requiring it. If omitted, the starter profile says UNKNOWN
and matching cannot treat an offer as present.

## Selected bounded slice

1. Replace the essay-stack interaction with one page containing three sections:
   what the member is doing, what is in the way, and what they already have.
   Required input is one short goal plus structured stage and blocker choices.
2. Capture attempted paths with explicit `not tried`, `tried`, `still trying`,
   and `ruled out` statuses. Preserve those statuses in the submitted packet and
   add P0 negation/history/unknown tests before accepting the build.
3. Add an editable/readable working brief and member-word explanation data for
   Recommendations. A ranking without a cited answer remains general, not
   personalized.

## Not in this slice

- durable account custody or schema/RLS;
- live member matching or introductions;
- professional advice, eligibility, or provider verification;
- push, deploy, secrets, spending, or SQL.
