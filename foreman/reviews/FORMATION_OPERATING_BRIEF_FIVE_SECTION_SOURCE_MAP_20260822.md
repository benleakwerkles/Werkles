## Safe five-section candidate using only current topics

1. **What we are testing** — purpose, first customer/use case, 30-day test.
2. **Who owns which work** — responsibilities and decision rights.
3. **What each person may contribute** — contributions, with adviser boundary.
4. **What must be checked before a bigger promise** — money questions and
   proof needs; questions only, no generated answer.
5. **What remains sensitive or unresolved** — exit, IP, confidentiality, and
   unknowns only when exact accepted text exists; otherwise the section stays
   absent rather than auto-filled.

## Composition rule

generated practice answer, untouched suggested joint wording, objection note,
or event history may be used to make the brief appear complete.
