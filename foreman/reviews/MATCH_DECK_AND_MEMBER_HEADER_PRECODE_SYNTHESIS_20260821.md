## Pre-code rulings

### R1 — Current four questions are rejected

The current generator performs label interpolation, not conversation design.
`Chair space`, `Funds not verified`, and `Ownership` are internal fragments
exposed as if they were understood concepts. The generic day-to-day question
does not demonstrate any knowledge of the candidate.

A member-facing question must bind all four of these inputs:

1. one specific member goal, constraint, attempted path, asset, or decision;
2. one specific candidate offer, experience, ask, or stated uncertainty;
3. the relationship between those two facts;
4. a decision, test, or unknown the conversation could genuinely advance.

If any required input is absent, the engine returns a plain honest fallback or
withholds the question. It must not expose a raw label to simulate specificity.

### R2 — Person-specific means materially different

Changing only a name, city, lane label, or category noun does not create a new
conversation. Two candidate question sets are distinct only when the practical
decision, relevant contribution, or uncertainty differs.

### R3 — Strongest honest fit remains first

Contribution diversity may operate only among candidates that already satisfy
the authoritative eligibility, suppression, and minimum-reason rules. It may
not admit an irrelevant candidate, revive a ruled-out path, override the
strongest honest fit, or rank Backers by account value.

### R4 — Fewer honest results beat padded repeats

Two defensible possibilities are better than three with a cloned archetype.
The short result must look intentional:

> We found two people worth exploring right now. We left out weaker repeats.

The member then receives one useful next action: improve the missing profile
signal, change a location preference, or begin one candidate-specific practice
conversation. The interface must not imply that more candidates exist but are
hidden behind payment.

### R5 — Geography is a preference, not proof of fit

Same-city/state may break a genuine near-tie or satisfy an explicit member
preference. It may not manufacture eligibility or bury a materially stronger
candidate. Two Virginia profiles are not automatically wrong; they are wrong if
the engine cannot explain why both contribute differently or if geography
suppressed stronger useful variety.

### R6 — The header is two-tier, not swapped

Every ordinary page retains the same public Werkles identity and public
navigation. Authenticated or honestly identified local-preview member mode adds
a second row beneath it containing stable member destinations. It does not
replace the public row.

Required member destinations remain stable:

- Member Home

- My Bellows
- Crucible
- Profile
- Update Intake / Review Answers
- Sign out when a real sign-out action exists

The final information architecture and density require Ender/Petra review; the
implementation may not silently prune destinations solely to make the row fit.

  turning information bubbles into controls.
